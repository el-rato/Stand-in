Terms

Effective 1 Sep 2026. Plain words, no traps.

1. The deal. Askers pay doers to film legal, public-place tasks on video. Every order shows its total upfront — no hidden fees, ever.

2. Our cut. 20% platform fee on completed orders (80% to the doer), 50% of location licenses. Subscriptions are monthly, cancel anytime in two taps.

3. Escrow + refunds. Payment is held until delivery. No video, or no pickup: automatic full refund. Cancel an open order anytime for an instant refund.

4. Payouts. Doer earnings release on asker approval and batch every Friday via Stripe. Missed payouts earn the next month free.

5. Acceptable use. Legal tasks only, public places only. No addresses, meetups, harassment, or anything requiring breaking rules. Violations end accounts.

6. Content. Doers grant buyers a personal license to their delivered video; brands need a licensing plan for commercial reuse.

7. Accounts. One account per human, keep credentials secret. We may suspend accounts for fraud, chargebacks abuse, or safety violations.

8. Liability. The platform connects humans; doers act independently. Liability is capped at the fees paid in the prior 12 months.

9. Changes. Material changes get 14 days notice in-app. Continued use means acceptance.
